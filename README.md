## What Problem Does Farmer Buddy Solve?

Farmers often need quick, practical guidance about crop health, pests, soil, weather, and day-to-day farming decisions. Finding reliable advice in a familiar language can be difficult, especially when a problem needs to be identified from a crop image or understood in the context of local field conditions.

Farmer Buddy brings these needs together in one accessible farming assistant. It helps farmers understand crop-related problems earlier, make more informed decisions, and receive guidance in their native Indian language using their questions, crop images, location, weather, and soil conditions.

## Features

- **AI farming assistant** for questions about crops, pests, diseases, weather, soil, and general farming.
- **Crop image analysis** to support crop identification, health checks, and early disease or issue detection.
- **Location-based weather information** with relevant conditions such as temperature, humidity, pressure, dew point, wind, and local time.
- **Soil condition information** including pH, nitrogen, organic carbon, clay, sand, silt, CEC, and bulk density.
- **Context-aware recommendations** that combine the farmer’s question with available image, weather, and soil information.
- **Native-language responses** for a more accessible experience for Indian farmers.
- **Text and voice input** for asking questions naturally.
- **Conversation history** so signed-in users can revisit previous analyses and advice.
- **User profiles** with location, language, and profile image personalization.
- **Guest analysis** so visitors can try the farming assistant before signing in.
- **Admin review dashboard** for monitoring conversations that may need additional attention and reviewing detailed crop-analysis information.
